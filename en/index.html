OUR STORY

Since 1972

A sushi counter on the old pass of Yaen Toge

野猿峠　味の関所

Since 1972, we have served sushi and kaiseki (a multi-course Japanese meal) on Yaen Kaido road in Hachioji, western Tokyo.

Our chefs prepare traditional Japanese dishes with seasonal seafood. Take a seat at the counter and watch your nigiri being made, one piece at a time.

Dietary Needs

Vegetarian, religious or allergy needs? Just ask. As a small, independent restaurant, our chefs can often adjust dishes to suit your order.

  • Tell us when you book or order — for example, no meat, no pork, no alcohol, or a food allergy.
  • Vegetable options include cucumber, pickle and natto rolls, seasonal vegetable tempura and seaweed salad.
  • Please note: many dishes use soy sauce (soy, wheat), dashi (fish stock) and mirin (sweet rice wine). We do not hold halal or other certifications, so please tell us what you need and we will do our best.
